FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most commonly asked questions about our digital government services platform.

How do I register on the Stackly portal?
Visit the Sign Up page and fill in your full name, email address, mobile number, and create a password. Verify your mobile via OTP and your Aadhaar number for identity confirmation. Registration takes less than 3 minutes.
Which documents are accepted for online verification?
We accept Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, and Driving License for identity verification. For address proof: utility bills, bank statements, or rent agreements not older than 3 months are accepted. Documents must be uploaded in PDF or JPG format (max 2MB each).
How can I track my application status?
After submitting an application, you'll receive a unique Application Reference Number (ARN). Log into your Citizen Dashboard and navigate to "My Applications" to see real-time status. You'll also receive SMS and email updates at every processing milestone.
Is the platform available in regional languages?
Yes! Stackly is available in 12 regional languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Urdu, and Assamese. Switch language from the top navigation bar or profile settings.
What payment methods are accepted for government fees?
We accept all major payment methods: Net Banking, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), NEFT/RTGS, and Government e-Challan. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.

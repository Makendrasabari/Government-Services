Who We Are

Building a Transparent, Connected Government

Stackly is India's flagship Digital Government Services Portal, launched under the Digital India initiative. We bridge the gap between citizens and government by offering a unified, secure, and accessible platform for all public services.

Founded in 2019, we have grown to serve 2.4 crore citizens across 28 states and 8 union territories, processing over 14,000 applications daily with an average satisfaction rating of 4.8/5.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

2019 — Launch

Stackly Portal Launched

Officially launched under Digital India with 12 core services across 5 pilot states.

2020 — Expansion

Nationwide Rollout

Expanded to all 28 states. Integrated with DigiLocker and Aadhaar authentication.

2021 — Innovation

AI Processing Engine Deployed

Launched AI-powered document verification reducing processing time by 70%.

2022 — Scale

1 Crore Citizens Milestone

Crossed 1 crore registered citizens. Launched mobile app in 12 regional languages.

2023 — Awards

National Digital Excellence Award

Recognised as India's best e-governance platform at the National e-Governance Awards.

2026 — Future

Blockchain & AI 2.0 Integration

Launched blockchain-secured certificates and advanced AI chatbot serving 24/7 support.
